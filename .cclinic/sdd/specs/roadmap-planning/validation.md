# roadmap-planning: validation

Acceptance criteria from the user's point of view.

| ID | Covers | User need | Accepted when |
|---|---|---|---|
| VAL-PLAN-001 | FR-PLAN-001, FR-PLAN-002, FR-PLAN-003 | I can plan a roadmap with one command | `cclinic plan` starts a chat with the PM in my terminal, or tells me clearly what's missing |
| VAL-PLAN-002 | FR-PLAN-004 | I choose what the PM plans | I'm asked whether to create a new roadmap, extend one of my roadmaps (which I'm shown), or create a single task |
| VAL-PLAN-003 | FR-PLAN-005, FR-PLAN-016 | Only the PM, with my approval, shapes my roadmaps | When cclinic runs the PM, it can read my project and the web but can't change anything, and roadmaps, milestones and roadmap tasks can't be created other than from a proposal I approved |
| VAL-PLAN-004 | FR-PLAN-006, FR-PLAN-007, FR-PLAN-008 | Planning feels like a conversation with a PM who knows my project | I write in my own words, the PM replies with what it found and a few clear questions, I can pick a suggested answer or write my own, and I see where its outside research came from |
| VAL-PLAN-005 | FR-PLAN-009, FR-PLAN-010 | A plan that can't run is never shown to me as ready | Proposals with missing tasks, bad dependencies or loops are fixed by the PM before I see them, and a PM that stops replying ends the run without saving a roadmap and without losing my session |
| VAL-PLAN-006 | FR-PLAN-011, FR-PLAN-012, FR-PLAN-013 | I approve the roadmap before it's saved | I see the whole roadmap with its dependencies, can keep chatting until I'm happy, and after I approve it's saved in full and I'm told how to run it |
| VAL-PLAN-007 | FR-PLAN-014, FR-PLAN-015 | Planning can't cost too much or leave a half-saved plan | Cancelling, stopping or hitting a limit saves no roadmap, a run stops at my Claude plan's limit or its turn limit, and I'm told how to resume unless I cancelled |
| VAL-PLAN-008 | FR-PLAN-017, FR-PLAN-018, FR-PLAN-019, FR-PLAN-020 | I can have the PM write one well-defined task without planning a whole roadmap | Task mode chats with me about the change, proposes one task with its spec and dependencies, standalone or in a roadmap I choose, and saves it only after I approve |
| VAL-PLAN-009 | FR-PLAN-021, FR-PLAN-022, FR-PLAN-023, FR-PLAN-024 | I can explain my idea with what I already have | I start by describing my idea, and can attach screenshots, mockups, briefs or other documents when I want to; files the PM can't use are refused with a clear reason, and my originals are never changed or committed |
| VAL-PLAN-010 | FR-PLAN-025, FR-PLAN-026, FR-PLAN-027, FR-PLAN-028, FR-PLAN-029 | The PM does its homework and helps me decide | The PM reads my project and files before asking, doesn't ask what they already answer, researches what's best when it matters, explains options with a recommendation, answers my questions, and checks its understanding with me before proposing |
| VAL-PLAN-011 | FR-PLAN-030, FR-PLAN-031, FR-PLAN-032, FR-PLAN-043 | I don't lose a planning conversation | If I close the terminal, the GUI or Claude Code, hit a limit or stop, I can pick the session up later from where it was, I'm always told how, and two places can't drive the same session at once |
| VAL-PLAN-012 | FR-PLAN-033, FR-PLAN-034, FR-PLAN-035, FR-PLAN-036 | Planning in the GUI is a chat that fits the rest of the GUI | The Plan panel opens straight to a chat that looks and feels like a coding agent's chat in the GUI's own light and dark styles: an empty conversation to type into, and one message box where I attach files, pick what to plan and the model from small pills, and send with an arrow; no form to fill in first |
| VAL-PLAN-013 | FR-PLAN-037, FR-PLAN-038, FR-PLAN-039 | The conversation is easy to read and act on | My messages and the PM's are clearly apart, its text is nicely formatted, I see when it's working and what it's doing and can stop it, and its proposal is a clear card I can approve with one click |
| VAL-PLAN-014 | FR-PLAN-040, FR-PLAN-041 | Everyone can use the chat, and problems are clear | I can use it by keyboard and screen reader, it doesn't jump while I read older messages, and errors appear where they happened with a way to fix them |
| VAL-PLAN-015 | FR-PLAN-042, FR-PLAN-044, FR-PLAN-045, FR-PLAN-046 | The PM is one real Claude session I can talk to anywhere | I can plan inside Claude Code with the PM skill and approve the plan there, continue the same conversation in the GUI or the terminal, or open a GUI session in Claude Code, and the GUI always shows the same conversation live, including what the PM is reading and searching |
